=========================================================================

  hits (vicriviroc, pantoprazole); --min-ct 2 produced 1 (epothilone) in the
  pre-fix local-kit run (formal runner: 0, a gene-order artefact).
  ALL of them vanish on the real 45956-signature library. Together with the
  gene-order artefact (_minct2_order_diagnosis.txt) and the nperm=1000/2000/
  3000 instability (_lincs_three_runs_comparison.txt), those nominal hits are
  benchmark FRAGILITY evidence only, never connectivity discoveries.
  Observed minimal-p (count=0) columns across all 6 calibrations: 19-61,
  far below the 460 needed for even one FDR<0.05 hit.

 INTERPRETATION / MANUSCRIPT USE
=========================================================================
1. §3.6 may now state that battery #5 used the REAL full LINCS L1000
   Level-5 database (45956 signatures), not a synthetic or 100-column ref.
   It must ALSO state the resolution floor: do not upgrade the claim to
   'no drug connectivity exists' or 'we discovered a connectivity signal'.
2. No FDR<0.05 hit in ANY of the 6 calibrations (3 specs x 2 pipelines).
   The reportable pattern is pipeline/aggregation sensitivity of the ranked
   top compounds and |score| (primary naive 0.008 vs rigorous 0.018;
   minct2 rigorous 0.064), with min q staying 0.3765-1.0000 throughout.
3. '0 significant' is CONSTRUCTIVE: m=45956, n_perm=2000, p_min=4.9975e-4,
   BH rank-1 threshold 1.0880e-6; >=460 tied minimal-p columns are needed.
   Report these four numbers explicitly. To make even the rank-1 threshold
   attainable by permutation alone, p_min <= 1.0880e-6 requires
   n_perm >= 919,117 (~9.2e5, i.e. ~460x the present calibration cost),
   which is computationally prohibitive at 45956 signatures. The resolution
   ceiling is therefore a PROPERTY OF THE AUDIT DESIGN, not a tuning issue.
4. Scores in lincs_calibration_*.csv are SIGNED; lincs_summary.txt prints
   |score| for its top compound. This report ranks by |score| and shows the
   signed value - do not quote the two files interchangeably.
5. q_value is rounded to 4 dp while significance uses the raw q; 0 rows in
   the full-library runs show the q==0.05 / significant=TRUE artefact.
6. Limitations to declare: n_perm=2000 p-resolution; signature-level (45956)
   rather than collapsed compound-level reference; the query-equivalent crop
   is exact for THIS estimator but is not a general full-matrix export;
   --gene-id entrez mapping follows the formal runner (9 unmapped native
   Entrez IDs, none in the crop); tie-order dependence is far smaller than
   in the subset (max tie group 15 vs ~95) but nonzero (2/200 columns with
   >100 repeated values) - the query-name sorting hardening proposed in
   PROJECT_STATE.md §9.5 remains outstanding and was intentionally NOT
   applied, so §3 stays bit-for-bit reproducible.
